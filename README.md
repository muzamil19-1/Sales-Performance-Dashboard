# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance using Power Query and DAX.
### Key Insights

1. **Revenue Concentration:** A small number of cities contribute a large portion of total revenue, indicating strong regional sales concentration.

2. **Top Product Performance:** A few products generate the majority of sales, highlighting the importance of focusing on high-performing product categories.

3. **Profitability Differences:** Some products have high revenue but lower profit margins, suggesting higher costs or lower pricing efficiency.

4. **Customer Contribution:** A limited number of customers account for a significant share of total revenue, showing the impact of key customers on business performance.

5. **Sales Trend Over Time:** Sales fluctuate across months, revealing seasonal demand patterns that can help in better inventory and sales planning.
